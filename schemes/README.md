# Electromechanical schematics

One or more schematic diagrams (PNG, JPEG or PDF) showing every electronic
component and motor and how they are connected.

**Status: pending.** The component list is not final — the drive motor, battery
and regulation are unchosen, and they are the parts a schematic exists to
document. See [`docs/1_mobility.md`](../docs/1_mobility.md) and the power-budget
section of [`docs/2_power_and_sensors.md`](../docs/2_power_and_sensors.md#6-power-budget).

The wiring that **is** fixed — I2C bus, multiplexer channel map, servo pin — is
documented in [`docs/2_power_and_sensors.md`](../docs/2_power_and_sensors.md).

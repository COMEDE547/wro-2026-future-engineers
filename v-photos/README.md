## These replaced an earlier set, and why

The previous six photos were taken **2026-08-05** and showed the vehicle before
the chassis was redesigned. The wheel-shed failure of 2026-08-06 was answered by
a ground-up redesign of the LEGO space-frame — stiffer than the first layout —
and every run and every committed artifact since 2026-08-09 is on that frame
([1 — Mobility](../docs/1_mobility.md) §3,
[D8](../docs/4_systems_and_decisions.md)). Photographs of a superseded frame
would have described a vehicle that no longer exists, so they were replaced
rather than kept.

The 2026-08-05 set also predated the Pi 5 and camera being mounted; both are
fitted and visible here.
